### ✅ DECISION: Option 1 — Detour via I-15 North

**Rationale:** Option 2 violates a hard constraint (`must_see_parks: Bryce`). The $100 fuel surcharge is absorbed in the budget.

**Mitigation:** Travelers will take a short rest break at Cedar City (~halfway) and arrive Bryce ~10 PM MST. Bryce Lodge check-in will be arranged as late check-in. No restaurant dinner reservation on this evening (pack snacks/road food from Zion).

|---|---|---|
