- 🚇 **Blue Line subway** connects O'Hare Airport directly to Wicker Park and the Loop — no rideshare needed, saves money

- 🧃 **Shared kitchen** at Wicker Park Budget Stay means you can grab groceries for a cheap breakfast on Day 3
- 🥶 **February in Chicago** — pack layers, the Riverwalk can be cold and windy
